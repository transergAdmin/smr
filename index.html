The Problem

Pigeons Are More Than a Nuisance in Pune

  • Spreads disease. Droppings carry Histoplasmosis and Salmonella — serious infections transmitted through airborne spores.
  • Damages property. Corrodes paint, AC units, solar panels and furniture — costing thousands in annual repairs.
  • Affects health. Feathers and dry droppings trigger breathing problems and allergies, especially in children and the elderly.
  • Creates fire hazards. Nests block drainage pipes and accumulate near electrical wiring, posing a real fire risk.
SMR Safety Nets provides a permanent, humane solution — without harming the birds.
